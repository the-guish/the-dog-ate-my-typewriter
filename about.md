## FAQ

**How are pages counted?**
As if the post were typed on a classic typewriter: US Letter paper, pica type,
double-spaced, with 1-inch margins. That is 27 lines of 65 characters per page.

**How is reading time estimated?**
The post's words, divided by 238 words per minute, the average adult reading
speed for non-fiction ([Brysbaert, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jml.2019.104047)).
Code is not counted.

The [tool that measures both](https://github.com/the-guish/the-dog-ate-my-typewriter/tree/main/notebooks/tools)
is in the repository, with its sources.
